Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our XRP lending platform.

XRP lending lets you provide your XRP to verified borrowers in exchange for monthly interest. It’s a safe and effortless way to earn passive income on your digital assets.

Earn a fixed 5% monthly interest, equivalent to 60% annual returns (APR). Interest is credited automatically to your main wallet on the platform.

The minimum amount required to start lending is 1,000 XRP.

To begin lending, register on the platform, verify your email, and deposit at least 1,000 XRP. Once deposited, your funds are automatically and securely allocated to verified borrowers.

You will receive your first daily interest reward 24 hours after your deposit. After that, earnings are credited daily at a 5% monthly rate.

We prioritize your security. All borrowers undergo strict screening, loans are overcollateralized, and advanced risk-management systems are in place. Additionally, part of the platform’s revenue funds an insurance reserve designed to protect lenders in the rare event of a default.

Yes, you have full access to your funds at any time. You can withdraw your principal and any accrued interest directly from your dashboard without any penalties or restrictions.

We don’t charge any deposit or withdrawal fees. Only a small portion of your earned interest is taken as a service fee to support platform operations.
